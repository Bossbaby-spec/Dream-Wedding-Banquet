Our Story

A wedding venue Delhi families return to.

For over twenty-five years, Dream Wedding Banquets has been the setting for Delhi's most talked-about celebrations. From the red-carpet entrance to the last dance, every detail is handled by one team, so your family can simply enjoy the day.

Our in-house decor studio designs mandaps, stages and entrances around your tradition. Our kitchens serve pure vegetarian and multi-cuisine menus, and our event managers stay with you from the first tasting to the vidaai.

Questions

Before you visit.

How far in advance should we book?

For peak wedding months, 8 to 12 months ahead is safest. For other dates, 2 to 3 months is usually enough.

Can we bring our own caterer or decorator?

Our in-house kitchen and decor studio are recommended, and outside vendors are allowed by arrangement. Please ask the wedding desk for details.

Do you offer a tasting before we book?

Yes. We arrange a menu tasting for confirmed enquiries, so you can choose with confidence.

What is the music and timing policy?

Music follows local regulations and is permitted until 10 PM. Baraat and pheras timings are planned with our manager.

How do we confirm a date?

A date is held once an advance is paid and a written agreement is shared. Your wedding manager explains every term before you pay.
